WHY THIS EXISTS

The hardware is fine. The experience is broken.

Too many great machines come wrapped in software that fights you. A 3D printer that needs a Raspberry Pi and a firmware conversion before it'll tell you the bed temp. A car packed with sensors you can't see. A factory floor full of data trapped in PLCs.

So this studio builds the missing layer: one-click installers and open hardware that make the gear do what it always should have. No terminals. No pip install for end users. No telemetry phoning home. Honest about what works and what doesn't — every product ships with a Known Issues page on day one.

It chews through firmware bugs vendors won't fix, walks past the "this can't be done" forum threads, and ships the polished thing anyway. It's one developer across a lot of disciplines, which is exactly why the polish bar stays high: if it's not good enough to put a name behind, it doesn't ship.
