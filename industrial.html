Maguire blender integration

We speak fluent Maguire.

Maguire gravimetric weigh-batch blenders are workhorses — but the good data lives behind a proprietary protocol, and the controller only shows it two lines at a time on a keypad LCD. We pull it out: real consumption, real batch weights, real cost-per-shift — off the actual hardware, onto a dashboard, and into your ERP.

The data is in there. Getting it out is the job.

A Maguire's Modbus TCP port isn't a normal Modbus device — it's a wrapper around Maguire's own MLAN protocol. Point standard register tools at it and you get noise. We mapped the real path: MLAN command codes (Get Totals, Cycle Weight, Batch Info) tunnelled inside the Modbus packets, plus a careful parse of the keypad LCD buffer for the fields the protocol won't hand you directly. The result is honest numbers — and where the firmware genuinely can't report a value live, we label it a timestamped snapshot instead of faking it.

Beyond Maguire

This isn't a one-off. We live on the plant floor.

Machine troubleshooting portals

Private, plant-dedicated portals — one home screen, every machine behind it. Pick a line and get its troubleshooter and PLC configuration side by side, whether it runs a WAGO PLC or a Yaskawa one. A technician's tool, not a brochure.

Deep motion-machine troubleshooter

Fault search, a full I/O map, a live PLC monitor over Modbus, the schematic on-screen, and a KB built from the machine's ladder. We've found a marginal braking resistor by reading the run-enable edge — not reciting an alarm number.

Electrical print generation (JIC)

Letter-landscape drawings with a proper zone border, title block, BOM and notes — standard JIC ladder symbols. Give us the control scheme; we hand back a client-ready print package with operation and sensor guides.

PLC reverse-engineering

Inherited 1,000+ steps of uncommented ladder? We monitor the live logic while operating the machine, decode each bit/timer/register, and round-trip the labels into the project's device comments — a documented program.

Plant-knowledge AI search

A local semantic search across ~1,000 plant PDFs — PLCs, drives, heat controllers, meters. Ask in plain English, get the exact manual and page back. Runs entirely on-prem; nothing leaves the building.
