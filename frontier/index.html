EVE Frontier — Cycle 7 Stillness — two live mods

The frontier, on the record.

Two free mods built by HoneyBadger from EVE Frontier's public chain. War Room turns every kill and gate jump that reaches the chain into live PvP intel. Bounty Board puts a price on any pilot and pays the hunter straight from the chain.

// 01  WAR ROOM

War Room

A free PvP intel hub for EVE Frontier, built only from what the public chain publishes. Open it in a browser; nothing to install.

Every frame above is a capture of the live War Room on 21 Oct 2026. The numbers were real at that moment.

What a player gets
01

Live Map

See where the fighting is. Kill activity is drawn on the real star map, fog-of-war aware: if the chain does not publish a location, War Room does not invent one.

02

Kills board

Every kill that reaches the chain, the hottest systems, and today's player gate jumps. Each kill says how late it arrived, so you know how fresh the picture is.

03

Pilot dossiers

Look anyone up by name. Kills and losses, other characters on the same wallet, and when that wallet last signed an on-chain action. It is a record of what happened, not a tracker of where someone is.

04

Watch & AlertsLive — on the page

Watch pilots, systems or tribes. The moment a watched thing appears on chain, an alert lands on screen, with sound if you want it. Relaying those alerts to Discord is coming.

05

My Fleet & Fleet roomMy Fleet live

My Fleet reads your own structures and their fuel clocks from your wallet address, read-only. The shared Fleet room runs in demo mode until its rooms server is deployed.

06

Bounties

Most Wanted, expired and claimed bounties from the Bounty Board, read from the chain.

07

History

A 24 hour and 7 day replay scrubber, so you can see how a fight built up, not just how it ended.

08

Compact overlay

An in-game-sized view at #/overlay to keep beside the client. It only shows information; it never touches the game.

// 02  BOUNTY BOARD

Bounty Board

Put a price on any pilot. Whoever gets the kill collects straight from the chain.

Bounty Board is a public wanted list for EVE Frontier. Any player can put a price on any pilot by name. The reward locks on chain in SUI or EVE, and whoever gets the kill collects it straight from the chain, with the game's own killmail as proof.

No fee and no middleman. The reward sits in the bounty contract, not with HoneyBadger, and the contract pays the hunter directly. The poster pays only the network gas.

Nothing to install besides EVE Vault, the wallet you sign with. Anyone can read the board; you only need Vault to post or collect.

It runs on Cycle 7 Stillness, which lives on Sui testnet: rewards are test tokens, not real money.

How a bounty pays
01

Pick a pilot

Type any pilot's name or character ID.

02

Lock the reward

Choose the amount in SUI or EVE and how long it runs, then sign in EVE Vault. The reward locks in the hb_bounty contract on chain.

03

Get the kill

Whoever kills that pilot while the bounty is open has earned it. The game's own killmail is the proof; nobody has to vouch for anyone.

04

Collect

The hunter claims straight from the chain and gets the whole reward. No fee.

05

If nobody does

When a bounty runs out unclaimed, the poster can take it back. A kill made before it ran out can still collect until they do.

// 03  HOW IT IS BUILT

How it is built

Public data in, honest picture out.

01

Public chain only

Everything both mods show comes from EVE Frontier's public chain. War Room needs no login and no wallet to read.

02

Read-only, no inputs

Neither mod sends a keystroke or a click to the game. No automation, one human input per client. The only thing that ever writes to the chain is a bounty you post or collect yourself, signed in EVE Vault.

03

The kill-delay rule

Kills reach the chain late, minutes to days after they happen. War Room measures the median delay on the kills it has loaded and prints it, places each kill when it happened, and shows how late it arrived, every time.

04

No guessing

War Room never claims who is online or where anyone is right now. A last-seen time is only a last-seen time, and a hidden location stays hidden.

05

Sui testnet, Cycle 7

Both mods run against Stillness, the Cycle 7 world on Sui testnet, with test tokens. The Bounty Board's Move contract, hb_bounty, is published on testnet_stillness for that world.
